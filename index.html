Short Biography:

I obtained my Ph.D and MSE degrees in Computer and Information Science from the University of Pennsylvania in 2006 and 2001, respectively, and my Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Engineering with first-class honours from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). I was awarded the IEEE (Hong Kong Section) prize in 1999. Prior to joining the Hong Kong Baptist University, I was an Assistant Professor at School of Computer Engineering / Nanyang Technological University (NTU) for three years between 2005 and 2008. My prior research experience includes working as a research associate at the University of Edinburgh (2005), contributing to the Galax project at AT&T Labs Florham Park as a summer research intern, and visiting the HKUST theoretical computer science group at HKUST in 2003.

I have been elected as Fellow (FHKIE) of The Hong Kong Institution of Engineers (HKIE) - the Information discipline - and a standing committee member for Session 2026/27. I am a member of the Database Research Group at HKBU. I hold memberships with several professional associations, including professional member of ACM, senior member of IEEE, member of IEEE Computer Society and member of IEEE Technical Committee on Data Engineering (TCDE). I have been an associate editor of Distributed and Parallel Databases (DAPD) since March 2025. Since September 2021, I have been serving as the ACM SIGMOD information director. I have been recognized with several distinctions in roles throughout my career. In particular, I was the director of a Croucher Foundation Advanced Study Institute (ASI) titled "Frontiers in Big Data Graph Research" 2015. I have also served as a reviewer for the French Agence Nationale de Recherche (ANR) 2022, the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG; German Research Foundation) 2022, and the Austrian Science Fund (FWF) 2014. I served as a supervisor of Hong Kong Scholars Program (香江學者計劃) 2020.

I have received several awards recognizing my contributions to research, teaching and service. In particular, I was awarded a distinguished program committee (PC) member from ACM SIGMOD 2023 and 2021, respectively, a distinguished reviewer award from PVLDB 2019, an outstanding program committee member for IEEE ICDE 2025, and the best reviewers award from ACM CIKM 2021. I received a silver ring from DASFAA 2014 after serving as a session chair for multiple sessions. I was the recipient of the HKBU President's Award for Outstanding Performance in Individual Teaching 2022 and the HKBU President's Award for Outstanding Young Researcher 2016, HKBU Faculty Performance Award for Service 2026, the Department's Best Teaching Award in AY2020-2021 and AY2014-2015, Best Research Award in AY2023-2024 and AY2013-14, and Best Service Award in AY2024-25, AY2019-2020 and AY2017-2018, respectively.
