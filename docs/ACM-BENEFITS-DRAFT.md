# Draft: comments for ACM's SIGMOD benefits list (next update)

ACM publishes SIGMOD's member and community benefits at
https://www.acm.org/special-interest-groups/sigs/sigmod (currently "SIGMOD
Benefits for FY 2027"). Our own list is on https://www.sigmod.org/membership/
(`src/content/pages/membership/index.md`). In November 2026 we aligned our page
with ACM's list; these are the changes to send to ACM at the next update, so
the two lists match again.

Status: draft, not sent.

## Corrections

1. **DBLP link.** Replace `http://www.informatik.uni-trier.de/~ley/db/` with
   https://dblp.org/.
2. **Award name.** "The SIGMOD Most Reproducible Paper Award" is now
   "The SIGMOD Best Artifact Award".
3. **Links to sigmod.org.** Use the canonical addresses:
   - SIGMOD Online: https://www.sigmod.org/
   - DBCares: https://www.sigmod.org/sigmod-policies/dbcares-policy/
4. **Typo.** "Sigmod membership along with other ACM-level criteria … will
   also allow to receive a financial hardship waiver" → "SIGMOD membership,
   along with other ACM-level criteria, also makes authors eligible for a
   financial hardship waiver …".

## Additions

Benefits on our page that ACM's list does not have:

- **Student members:** eligibility for student travel grants.
- **Online members:**
  - SIGMOD/PODS conference proceedings (available before the conference) and
    the PACMMOD journal in the ACM Digital Library
    (https://dl.acm.org/conference/mod/proceedings,
    https://dl.acm.org/journal/pacmmod).
  - A bundle of SIGMOD-related publications made available through the ACM
    Digital Library.
  - Access to content from SIG sponsored and co-sponsored events in the ACM
    Digital Library.
  - A social networking presence to disseminate community news (ACM lists it
    under community benefits only).
- **Video series:** add its address,
  https://archive.sigmod.org/videoseries/.
- **Distinguished profiles:** the video interviews are now at
  https://archive.sigmod.org/publications/interview/.
