To become a member, see [Join SIGMOD](/join/). The members of SIGMOD are active in the development of new database technologies and techniques. As of July 2025, SIGMOD has 984 members with a significant international constituency. About 6.5% of the members are students. You can find ACM membership statistics [here](https://www.acm.org/special-interest-groups/volunteer-resources/sig-membership-stats/sigmod-membership-statistics). There are also active [ACM SIGMOD Chapters](/membership/sigmod-chapters/ "ACM SIGMOD Chapters") in some countries.

## Membership Benefits

There are two categories of membership, namely SIGMOD Online and SIGMOD Print, in addition to Student membership. The official list of benefits is on [ACM's SIGMOD page](https://www.acm.org/special-interest-groups/sigs/sigmod).

Online members receive:

-   Voting rights in ACM SIGMOD elections and the opportunity to serve as a SIGMOD officer
-   Discounted registration fees at events in-cooperation or sponsored by SIGMOD, including the SIGMOD/PODS conference
-   Announcements via the moderated, members-only DBWorld email distribution list, keeping members informed of the latest happenings in our field
-   The quarterly [SIGMOD Record](https://sigmodrecord.org/), which publishes articles, reports, and interviews that cover the most recent developments in the community (electronic edition)
-   [SIGMOD/PODS conference proceedings](https://dl.acm.org/conference/mod/proceedings) (available before the conference) and the [PACMMOD journal](https://dl.acm.org/journal/pacmmod) in the ACM Digital Library
-   A bundle of SIGMOD-related publications made available through the ACM Digital Library
-   Access to content from SIG sponsored and co-sponsored events in the ACM Digital Library
-   [A blog on “hot topics” in databases](https://blog.sigmod.org/)
-   The [SIGMOD video series](https://archive.sigmod.org/videoseries/), which includes lectures by senior members of our community on topics related to data management research
-   A social networking presence to disseminate community news

Print members receive:

-   All benefits of Online members, plus
-   SIGMOD Record print edition (4 issues per year)

Student members receive:

-   All benefits of Online Members
-   Eligibility for student travel grants

## Community benefits provided by SIGMOD

SIGMOD also provides these benefits to the whole community:

-   Access at SIGMOD Online to a range of content, including the following:
    -   Information about SIGMOD
    -   A blog on “hot topics” in databases
    -   Database resources (e.g., public domain software, list of graduating PhD students)
    -   Historical materials collected by or commissioned by SIGMOD, including [video interviews with distinguished members of the community](https://archive.sigmod.org/publications/interview/)
-   A financial hardship waiver of the article processing charge (APC) for PACMMOD authors who cannot afford it, based on SIGMOD membership along with other [ACM-level criteria](https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/policy-on-discretionary-open-access-apc-waivers)
-   A social networking presence to disseminate community news
-   The [DBCares committee](/sigmod-policies/dbcares-policy/), which aims to create an inclusive and diverse database community with zero tolerance for abuse, discrimination, or harassment
-   The [DnI initiative](https://dni.sigmod.org/), which coordinates efforts to increase diversity and inclusion in the data management research community
-   Support for [DBLP](https://dblp.org/) – a bibliography of computer science publications
-   Sponsorship of the annual SIGMOD and PODS conferences; co-sponsorship of DEBS, SIGKDD, SOCC, WSDM, and other conferences/workshops on occasion; sponsorship of the workshops held at the annual SIGMOD/PODS conference
-   [Reproducibility evaluation](https://reproducibility.sigmod.org/) of papers published at the ACM SIGMOD Conference
-   [Research Highlights](/sigmod-awards/sigmod-research-highlights/): a collection of best papers on data management that are relevant to the larger computer science community
-   [The SIGMOD awards](/sigmod-awards/): Edgar F. Codd Innovation Award, the Contributions Award, the Hector Garcia-Molina Distinguished Mentorship Award, the Systems Award, the Best Paper Award, the Test-of-Time Best Paper Award, the Jim Gray Dissertation Award, the Best Demonstration Award, and the Best Artifact Award
-   The SIGMOD Programming Contest
-   Undergraduate and graduate scholarships to enable undergraduate and graduate students to attend the SIGMOD/PODS conferences and other events on occasion
