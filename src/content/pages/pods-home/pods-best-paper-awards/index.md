Since 1997, PODS has given awards each year to the best papers published in PODS:

- **[Best Paper Award](#best-paper)** (since 1997): the best of all papers submitted, as judged by the program committee.
- **[Distinguished Paper Awards](#distinguished-paper)** (since 2025): a group of outstanding papers, as judged by the program committee.
- **[Best Student Paper Award](#best-student-paper)** (since 2009): the best submission, as judged by the program committee, written exclusively by a student or students. An author is considered a student if, at the time of submission, the author is enrolled in a program at a university or institution leading to a doctoral, master’s or bachelor’s degree. From PODS 2009 on, it replaced the Best Newcomer Award.
- **[Best Newcomer Award](#best-newcomer)** (1997–2008, and since 2024): the best submission, as judged by the program committee, written solely by authors who have never published in earlier PODS proceedings.

The program committee reserves the right to give both awards to the same paper, not to give an award, or to split an award among several papers. Papers authored or co-authored by program committee members are not eligible for an award.
