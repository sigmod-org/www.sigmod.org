Older SIGMOD publications and resources that are no longer updated are kept, as they were published, in the [SIGMOD Archive](https://archive.sigmod.org/):

-   [SIGMOD Digital Symposium Collection (DiSC)](https://archive.sigmod.org/publications/discs/): yearly DVD collections, 1999–2011
-   [The ACM SIGMOD Anthology](https://archive.sigmod.org/publications/anthology/): volumes 1–6, 1999–2007
-   [Distinguished Profiles in Databases](https://archive.sigmod.org/publications/interview/): video interviews with leading database researchers, 2001–2014
-   [SIGMOD Digital Review](https://archive.sigmod.org/publications/digital-review/index.html): reviews of notable database papers, 1999–2006
-   [SIGMOD/PODS Video Series](https://archive.sigmod.org/videoseries/): tutorial-style lectures, 2018
-   [DBLP mirror](https://archive.sigmod.org/publications/dblp/db/index.html): the DBLP pages from the Anthology discs, 1999 snapshot

Copies of past SIGMOD/PODS conference websites are at [mirror.sigmod.org](https://mirror.sigmod.org/).
