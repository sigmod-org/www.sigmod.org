Older SIGMOD publications that are no longer updated are kept in the [SIGMOD Archive](https://archive.sigmod.org/):

-   [SIGMOD Digital Symposium Collection (DiSC)](https://archive.sigmod.org/publications/discs/) (ended in 2011)
-   [Anthology](https://archive.sigmod.org/publications/anthology/) (ended in 2007)
-   [Distinguished Profiles](https://archive.sigmod.org/publications/interview/) (ended in 2014)
-   [SIGMOD Digital Review](https://archive.sigmod.org/publications/digital-review/index.html) (ended in 2006)
-   [SIGMOD/PODS Video Series](https://archive.sigmod.org/videoseries/) (2018)
-   [DBLP mirror](https://archive.sigmod.org/publications/dblp/db/index.html) (1999 snapshot)

Copies of past SIGMOD/PODS conference websites are at [mirror.sigmod.org](https://mirror.sigmod.org/).
