## Apache Flink

2023 SIGMOD Systems Award

The 2023 SIGMOD Systems Award goes to Apache Flink:

*“Apache Flink greatly expanded the use of stream data-processing.”*

The list of contributors listed for the award is just a selection from the list of [Flink committers](https://projects.apache.org/committee.html?flink). As an open-source project, Apache Flink received contributions from over 1400 contributors, many of them substantial, which are not listed below.
