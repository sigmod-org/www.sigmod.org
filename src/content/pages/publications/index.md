Since 2023, papers accepted at SIGMOD are published in the journal [Proceedings of the ACM on Management of Data (PACMMOD)](https://dl.acm.org/journal/pacmmod) and invited for presentation at the SIGMOD conference; before that, they appeared in the Proceedings of the ACM SIGMOD International Conference on Management of Data. Since 2024, papers accepted at PODS are also published in PACMMOD, instead of the Proceedings of the ACM PODS. PACMMOD issues are tagged as SIGMOD or PODS issues. More information is in [a note about the ranking and indexing of PACMMOD](/note-about-ranking-indexing-of-pacmmod/).

-   [Proceedings of the ACM on Management of Data (PACMMOD)](https://dl.acm.org/journal/pacmmod): SIGMOD papers since 2023, PODS papers since 2024
-   [SIGMOD Conference (1974-)](https://dl.acm.org/conference/mod)
-   [PODS Conference (1982-)](https://dl.acm.org/conference/pods)
-   [SIGMOD Record](https://sigmodrecord.org/ "SIGMOD Record")
-   [SIGMOD Blog](https://blog.sigmod.org/ "SIGMOD Blog")
-   [ACM TODS](https://dl.acm.org/journal/tods)
-   [SIGMOD @ ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/sig/sigmod)
-   [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/)

Earlier SIGMOD publications that are no longer updated, such as the [Digital Symposium Collection](https://archive.sigmod.org/publications/discs/), the [SIGMOD Anthology](https://archive.sigmod.org/publications/anthology/) and [Distinguished Profiles in Databases](https://archive.sigmod.org/publications/interview/), are in the [SIGMOD Archive](https://archive.sigmod.org/) (see also [the archive page](/archive/)).
