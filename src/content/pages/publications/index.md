Since 2023, papers accepted at SIGMOD or PODS are published in the journal [Proceedings of the ACM on Management of Data (PACMMOD)](https://dl.acm.org/journal/pacmmod) and invited for presentation at the conference; before that, they appeared in the SIGMOD and PODS conference proceedings. PACMMOD issues are tagged as SIGMOD or PODS issues. More information is in [a note about the ranking and indexing of PACMMOD](/note-about-ranking-indexing-of-pacmmod/).

-   [Proceedings of the ACM on Management of Data (PACMMOD)](https://dl.acm.org/journal/pacmmod): SIGMOD/PODS papers since 2023
-   [SIGMOD Conference (1974-)](https://dl.acm.org/conference/mod)
-   [PODS Conference (1982-)](https://dl.acm.org/conference/pods)
-   [SIGMOD Record](https://sigmodrecord.org/ "SIGMOD Record")
-   [SIGMOD Blog](https://blog.sigmod.org/ "SIGMOD Blog")
-   [ACM TODS](https://dl.acm.org/journal/tods)
-   [SIGMOD @ ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/sig/sigmod)
-   [ACM Digital Library](https://dl.acm.org/)

Some previous resources that are no longer supported or updated can be found on [the archive page](/archive/).
