The 2021 SIGMOD Systems Award goes to the Sloan Digital Sky Survey.

> *An early and influential demonstration of the power of data science to transform a scientific domain. Not only has this system shown off the value of data management technology, it has also influenced data management by publishing real analytic workloads that have been used for testing, comparing, and advancing data management systems.*
