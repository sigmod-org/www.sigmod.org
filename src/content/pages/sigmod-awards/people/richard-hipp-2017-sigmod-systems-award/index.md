**Dr. Richard Hipp** is the recipient of the 2017 SIGMOD Systems Award for SQLite, extending DBMS technology to low end devices.

**Details**: **Dr. Richard Hipp** is a Member of Technical Staff at  [Hwaci](http://www.hwaci.com/staff.html). He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Duke University, and spends most of his time solving tough technical problems for Hwaci’s clients. Richard is also available for training and staff development, especially in the areas of Unix/Linux programming, the [SQLite](http://www.sqlite.org/) database engine, and graphical programming using [Tcl/Tk](http://www.tcl.tk/).
