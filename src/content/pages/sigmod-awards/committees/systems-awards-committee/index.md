From 2015 to 2019, a separate SIGMOD Systems Award committee decided who receives the SIGMOD Systems Award. In 2020 it was folded into the [SIGMOD Awards Committee](/sigmod-awards/committees/sigmod-awards-committee/), which has selected the Systems Award recipients since. The committee members for 2015–2019 are listed below.
